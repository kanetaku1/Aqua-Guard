Status: Badge vs Indicator

One question decides it: is this the severity of the subject of the row, card or page, and is it Attention or worse? Yes → Badge. Everything else → Indicator. At most one Badge per row or card.
CriticalWarningAttentionStatus Badge — filled chip + severity icon. Only the subject severity: list Status column, Alert / Issue severity, page title (lg), Pond tile, Sensor tile
NormalNormalStatus Indicator — no surface, dot + regular text. Normal subject (lg next to a page title)
3 of 8 Ponds2 behindBreakdowns / secondary statuses — Farm Status aspects (Water quality · Growth · Operations), Health column
DraftSubmittedReport status — the urgency of a Draft is shown by the due time and the Continue button, not by a Badge
